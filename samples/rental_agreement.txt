RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made on 25-07-2026 at Coimbatore

BETWEEN

Mrs. Lakshmi Narayanan, residing at 3, Lake View Street, Saibaba Colony, Coimbatore - 641011 (hereinafter called the "Owner"),

AND

Mr. Arun Prakash, permanent address 17, Market Road, Erode - 638001 (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

1. PROPERTY
The Owner agrees to let out the 2BHK flat No. 4B, Sri Sai Apartments, Peelamedu, Coimbatore - 641004 (the "Premises") to the Tenant for residential use only.

2. TERM
The tenancy shall be for a period of eleven (11) months starting from 01-08-2026 and ending on 30-06-2027. It may be renewed with a 5% increase in rent by mutual consent.

3. RENT AND DEPOSIT
3.1 The monthly rent is Rs. 15,000 (Rupees Fifteen Thousand only), payable on or before the 5th of every month by bank transfer.
3.2 A late fee of Rs. 100 per day shall apply if rent is paid after the 10th of the month.
3.3 The Tenant has paid a refundable security deposit of Rs. 60,000, which shall be returned within 15 days of vacating, after deducting any unpaid dues or damage charges.

4. MAINTENANCE AND UTILITIES
4.1 Electricity and water charges shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual meter readings.
4.2 Monthly apartment maintenance of Rs. 1,500 shall be paid by the Tenant.
4.3 Major structural repairs shall be the responsibility of the Owner. Minor repairs such as bulbs, taps and fittings shall be handled by the Tenant.

5. TENANT OBLIGATIONS
5.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and in good condition.
5.2 The Tenant shall not sub-let the Premises or use it for any commercial or illegal purpose.
5.3 The Tenant shall not make structural changes without the Owner's written permission.
5.4 The Tenant shall allow the Owner to inspect the Premises with 24 hours' prior notice.

6. TERMINATION
6.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving one (1) month's written notice.
6.2 If the Tenant fails to pay rent for two consecutive months, the Owner may terminate the tenancy and the Tenant shall vacate within 15 days.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India and the Tamil Nadu Regulation of Rights and Responsibilities of Landlords and Tenants Act, 2017. Courts at Coimbatore shall have jurisdiction.
